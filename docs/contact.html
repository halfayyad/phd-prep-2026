Good to know

Shipping, returns & the rest

Orders leave within two business days. Standard delivery is $6 and takes three to five business days; express is $14 and takes one to two. Shipping is free on orders over $60.

Yes, to most countries. International rates are calculated at checkout and delivery takes seven to fourteen business days. Any customs duties are the recipient's responsibility.

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If you've opened something and it doesn't work for you, email us within 30 days — we'll usually refund it anyway. We'd rather you had the right product than the wrong one in a drawer.

Send a photo of your bare face in daylight, near a window, with no filter. We'll come back with a recommendation and a second choice. If the shade we pick is wrong, the return is on us.

Yes. Nothing is tested on animals by us, our laboratories or our ingredient suppliers, and we do not sell into markets that require animal testing as a condition of entry.
