About

We started with the products we kept running out of

Cédrisse began with a simple frustration: the products that got used to the bottom were never the ones with the biggest launches. They were the quiet ones — the tint that matched, the lipstick that didn't hurt, the balm that took everything off.

So that's the range. Twelve products, each one made because the version we wanted didn't exist at a price that made sense. No seasonal drops, no shade extensions announced a year late.

What we hold to

The standards behind every formula

Full range or nothing

A complexion product launches in its complete depth range or it doesn't launch. Deep shades are developed alongside the rest, not added after the complaints arrive.

Ingredients in the open

Every product page carries the full INCI list. Fragrance-free by default, and anything that could irritate is named rather than buried.

Cruelty-free, verified

No animal testing by us, our labs or our suppliers, and we don't sell into markets that require it. That rule costs us revenue and stays anyway.

How a product gets made

Slowly, and on real faces

Every formula is worn — for full days, in real light, by people with different skin types and depths — before it goes anywhere near production. If it pills under an SPF, breaks up by 4pm, or flashes back in a photo, it goes back to the lab.

That's why the range is short. A product only ships when it beats whatever we were already using.
