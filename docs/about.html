About

Hello — I'm the artist behind Cédrisse Beauty

I've been doing makeup professionally since 2019, and the thing I care about most hasn't changed since the first booking: you should recognise yourself in the mirror when I hand it to you.

My work sits on the quieter side of glam. I build skin rather than cover it, match undertone properly rather than approximately, and put the drama exactly where the light will find it. If you want something bolder, we'll go bolder — but it will still be built on skin that looks like skin.

What guides the work

Three things I won't compromise on

Every skin tone, properly

The kit carries a full depth and undertone range across every foundation, concealer and blush line I use. Nobody should be matched "close enough".

Hygiene without exception

Disposable applicators, decanted product, brushes sanitised between every face. Anyone with sensitive skin or allergies is patch-tested at the trial.

A calm morning

A written timeline, an early arrival and a realistic per-face estimate. The makeup should be the least stressful part of your day.
