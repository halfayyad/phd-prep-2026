The approach

Skin first. Always.

Great makeup is 70% preparation. Every appointment opens with a short skin consult — hydration, texture, tone — and a prep routine chosen for your skin on that day, not a fixed formula.

From there the makeup is built in thin, buildable layers: real coverage where it's needed, bare skin where it isn't. The result reads soft in person and holds its structure on camera.
